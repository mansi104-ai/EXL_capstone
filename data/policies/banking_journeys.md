# Gulf Union Bank — Journey Playbook (CBUAE Consumer Protection)

This playbook is organised by **banking journey** rather than by vulnerability
driver. It exists because the two previous documents answer "this customer is
vulnerable — now what?", while a handler on a live call is usually asking the
opposite question: "this is a collections call / a scam report / an estate —
what does the rulebook require of me here, and what changes because this customer
is vulnerable?"

Every clause names the obligation it derives from. Detection remains advisory;
every adaptation offered must be recorded. All figures and contact details are
illustrative.

**On the `Offer:` lines.** These are the sentences a handler — or the voice
agent — may actually say. `Offer:` is the approved English. `Offer[ar]:` and
`Offer[ur]:` are the approved Arabic and Urdu, authored here and signed off by
the same reviewer who signed off the clause. They are not translations produced
at call time, because wording generated on the call is wording nobody approved.
Where a language is missing below, the agent falls back to English and records
that it did: a gap for a reviewer to close, not a sentence for a model to invent.

## Driver: Resilience

### VP-J1 — Forbearance and due consideration (CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards)
Journeys: forbearance_request, arrears_collections, affordability_shock
Products: credit_card, personal_loan, overdraft, car_finance
Offer: I can reduce your instalment to something you can actually manage
Offer: I can freeze the interest and charges while we sort out a plan
Offer: I can accept a smaller payment for now, with no pressure to catch up quickly
Offer[ar]: يمكنني تخفيض القسط الشهري إلى مبلغ تستطيع سداده فعلاً
Offer[ar]: يمكنني تجميد الفوائد والرسوم إلى أن نتفق على خطة
Offer[ar]: يمكنني قبول دفعة أصغر في الوقت الحالي، دون أي ضغط للسداد بسرعة
Offer[ur]: میں آپ کی ماہانہ قسط کم کر سکتی ہوں تاکہ آپ آسانی سے ادا کر سکیں
Offer[ur]: جب تک ہم کوئی منصوبہ طے کرتے ہیں، میں سود اور چارجز روک سکتی ہوں
Offer[ur]: میں فی الحال کم رقم قبول کر سکتی ہوں، جلد ادائیگی کا کوئی دباؤ نہیں ہے
Where a customer is in or approaching arrears on a credit facility,
treat the customer with forbearance and due consideration. Consider, and offer
where suitable: suspending, reducing or deferring instalments; accepting token
payments for a reasonable period; freezing or reducing interest and charges; and
agreeing a sustainable repayment arrangement based on what the customer can
actually afford after essentials. Any restructured facility must still respect
the debt burden ratio in CBUAE Circular 29/2011 — a plan the customer cannot
carry is not forbearance, it is a deferred default. Do not press for a payment
the customer has said they cannot make, and do not treat a request for help as a
default event. Record the option offered, the option accepted, and the review
date.

### VP-J2 — Relief, independent advice and the ombudsman route
Journeys: forbearance_request, arrears_collections, affordability_shock
Offer: I can pause the interest, the fees and the contact for ninety days while you get advice
Offer: I can refer you to Sanadak, the free and independent ombudsman for banking
Offer[ar]: يمكنني تعليق الفوائد والرسوم والاتصال لمدة تسعين يوماً بينما تحصل على المشورة
Offer[ar]: يمكنني إحالتك إلى «سندك»، وحدة تسوية المنازعات المصرفية المستقلة والمجانية
Offer[ur]: میں نوے دن کے لیے سود، فیس اور رابطہ روک سکتی ہوں تاکہ آپ مشورہ لے سکیں
Offer[ur]: میں آپ کو «سندک» بھیج سکتی ہوں، جو بینکنگ کے لیے مفت اور خودمختار محتسب ادارہ ہے
Where a customer is in genuine financial difficulty, explain the bank's relief
period: an agreed window during which interest, fees and collections contact are
paused while the customer takes advice or assembles a plan. Explain Sanadak, the
independent Ombudsman Unit for banking and insurance, as free to the consumer and
available once the internal complaint route is exhausted, and never present a
fee-charging debt-settlement company as equivalent to it. Where the customer's
debts extend beyond this bank, explain that Federal Decree-Law 19/2019 provides a
court-supervised route to settle personal debts, and that this is a matter for a
qualified adviser rather than for this call. If a relief period is already in
force, stop all collections contact immediately.

### VP-J3 — Income and expenditure without pressure
Journeys: forbearance_request, affordability_shock, arrears_collections
Offer: We can do this over more than one call if that's easier
Offer: I can take the figures from your adviser instead, if you'd prefer
Offer[ar]: يمكننا إتمام ذلك على أكثر من مكالمة إذا كان ذلك أسهل لك
Offer[ar]: يمكنني أخذ الأرقام من مستشارك بدلاً من ذلك، إذا كنت تفضل
Offer[ur]: اگر آپ کے لیے آسان ہو تو ہم یہ کام ایک سے زیادہ کالوں میں مکمل کر سکتے ہیں
Offer[ur]: اگر آپ چاہیں تو میں یہ تفصیلات آپ کے مشیر سے لے سکتی ہوں
When assessing what a customer can afford, use a standard income-and-expenditure
review and allow essential costs — rent, DEWA or the local utility, school fees,
food, transport, childcare, disability-related costs, and any regular remittance
the customer's family depends on — before any credit repayment. A remittance is
not discretionary spending, and treating it as though it were is the commonest
way an affordability assessment in this market produces a figure the customer
will never meet. Offer to complete the review over more than one call, or to
accept a figure from the customer's adviser instead. Never ask a customer to
justify essential spending, and never propose a figure that leaves essentials
unmet.

### VP-J4 — Priority of essentials over our debt
Journeys: arrears_collections, affordability_shock, forbearance_request
Offer: Please pay your rent, your utilities and your food first — we can wait
Offer: I can stop the letters and the calls while we get this sorted
Offer[ar]: من فضلك ادفع الإيجار والفواتير والطعام أولاً — نحن يمكننا الانتظار
Offer[ar]: يمكنني إيقاف الرسائل والمكالمات إلى أن نحل هذا الأمر
Offer[ur]: پہلے اپنا کرایہ، بجلی پانی کے بل اور کھانا ادا کریں — ہم انتظار کر سکتے ہیں
Offer[ur]: جب تک یہ معاملہ حل نہیں ہو جاتا، میں خطوط اور کالیں بند کر سکتی ہوں
Where a customer indicates they are choosing between an essential bill and a
payment to us, our debt is non-priority. Say so explicitly, pause collections
activity, and move the conversation to support. Avoiding foreseeable harm takes
precedence over recovering the balance on this call.

## Driver: Life events

### VP-J5 — Bereavement: a single point of contact
Journeys: bereavement_estate, third_party_access
Products: current_account, savings, mortgage, personal_loan, credit_card
Offer: I can be your single point of contact, so you only have to tell me once
Offer: I can stop the interest, the charges and the letters from today
Offer: I only need to see the death certificate once
Offer[ar]: يمكنني أن أكون نقطة الاتصال الوحيدة لك، فلا تحتاج إلى شرح الأمر مرة أخرى
Offer[ar]: يمكنني إيقاف الفوائد والرسوم والرسائل اعتباراً من اليوم
Offer[ar]: أحتاج إلى رؤية شهادة الوفاة مرة واحدة فقط
Offer[ur]: میں آپ کے لیے واحد رابطہ کار بن سکتی ہوں، تاکہ آپ کو دوبارہ بتانا نہ پڑے
Offer[ur]: میں آج ہی سے سود، چارجز اور خطوط بند کر سکتی ہوں
Offer[ur]: مجھے موت کا سرٹیفکیٹ صرف ایک بار دیکھنا ہے
On notification of a death, open a bereavement case with one named point of
contact so the customer never repeats the disclosure. Suspend interest, charges,
arrears activity and marketing on the deceased's accounts from the date of
notification. Accept a death certificate copy once, and set out clearly which
documents are needed and which are not. Offer to deal with a representative and
allow the customer to set the pace — there is no deadline the customer must meet
on this call.

### VP-J6 — Estate, succession and joint holdings
Journeys: bereavement_estate
Products: mortgage, personal_loan, savings, current_account
Offer: I can explain plainly which documents we need and what happens at each step
Offer: I can pause the payments while everything is settled, and confirm that in writing
Offer[ar]: يمكنني أن أشرح لك بوضوح المستندات المطلوبة وما يحدث في كل خطوة
Offer[ar]: يمكنني تعليق الدفعات إلى أن تتم التسوية، وتأكيد ذلك كتابياً
Offer[ur]: میں سادہ الفاظ میں بتا سکتی ہوں کہ کون سے کاغذات درکار ہیں اور ہر مرحلے پر کیا ہوتا ہے
Offer[ur]: میں تصفیہ مکمل ہونے تک ادائیگیاں روک سکتی ہوں اور اس کی تحریری تصدیق کر سکتی ہوں
On a death, accounts in the deceased's sole name are held until the bank receives
a succession certificate from the competent court, or a will registered with a
recognised registry. State that process as fact, and state the documents needed.
**Do not state who inherits, in what shares, or under which law.** Succession in
the UAE turns on the deceased's religion, nationality and whether a registered
will exists; it is a legal question, it is decided by a court and not by this
bank, and answering it on a collections or bereavement call is giving legal
advice. Where accounts are held jointly, explain what the bank's own process is
for the surviving holder and refer the inheritance question to a qualified
person. Do not pursue a survivor for the deceased's sole debts. Where a mortgage
or personal loan is affected, offer reduced payments while the estate is settled,
check whether a credit life takaful or insurance benefit attaches to the
facility, and confirm the arrangement in writing.

## Driver: Capability

### VP-J7 — Unauthorised and induced payment fraud
Journeys: fraud_scam
Products: current_account, savings
Offer: I can stop any further payments going out right now
Offer: I can try to recall the money from the receiving bank
Offer: I can explain how and when you would get your money back
Offer[ar]: يمكنني إيقاف أي مدفوعات أخرى تخرج من حسابك الآن
Offer[ar]: يمكنني محاولة استرجاع المبلغ من البنك المستلم
Offer[ar]: يمكنني أن أشرح لك كيف ومتى ستستعيد أموالك
Offer[ur]: میں ابھی مزید ادائیگیاں روک سکتی ہوں
Offer[ur]: میں وصول کنندہ بینک سے رقم واپس منگوانے کی کوشش کر سکتی ہوں
Offer[ur]: میں بتا سکتی ہوں کہ آپ کو رقم کیسے اور کب واپس ملے گی
Where a customer reports that they were deceived into authorising a payment,
treat it as an unauthorised-transaction claim, not a customer error. Never
suggest moving money to a "safe account" — that instruction is the scam, and in
this market it usually arrives as a caller claiming to be from the bank, the
police or a courier company. Take the report without attributing blame, stop
further payments, attempt recall, and explain the reimbursement process and its
timescales. Assess whether the customer's circumstances made them more
susceptible and record it. Offer a fraud-support referral and, where the loss is
significant, a wellbeing check.

### VP-J8 — Third-party mandates, capacity and power of attorney
Journeys: third_party_access, bereavement_estate
Offer: I can set up formal permission so they can help you properly and safely
Offer: I'd still like to speak with you directly wherever you'd like me to
Where someone else operates the account, confirm the basis — a notarised power of
attorney (attested where it was executed abroad), a court-appointed guardian, a
third-party mandate, or informal help. Assume capacity unless there is evidence
otherwise, assess decision by decision, and never treat a diagnosis, an age or a
language barrier as proof of incapacity. A customer who needs an interpreter has
full capacity and is entitled to be spoken to directly. Where help is informal,
offer a formal arrangement that protects the customer, and speak to the customer
directly wherever they wish.

## Driver: Health

### VP-J9 — Addiction and harmful spending: controls before credit
Journeys: gambling_harm, product_sale
Products: current_account, credit_card, overdraft
Offer: I can block that category of payment on your card today
Offer: I can set a daily spending limit, and stop any credit offers reaching you
Offer: I can give you the number for the mental health support line on 800 4673
Where a customer indicates addiction or compulsive spending harm, offer the
practical controls first: a merchant-category block on the card, spending and ATM
limits, a cooling-off period on credit applications, and removal from credit
marketing. Do not offer additional credit, a limit increase, or a top-up loan,
and decline an inbound request for one with an explanation and an offer of
support instead. Signpost the national mental health support line (800 4673)
without judgement, and record the disclosure as a health driver.

### VP-J10 — Serious illness affecting a credit agreement
Journeys: forbearance_request, affordability_shock, arrears_collections
Products: mortgage, personal_loan, credit_card
Offer: I can reduce your instalment while you're unwell
Offer: I can check whether your account has cover that would help with this
Offer[ar]: يمكنني تخفيض القسط الشهري أثناء فترة مرضك
Offer[ar]: يمكنني التحقق مما إذا كان حسابك يشمل تأميناً يساعد في هذه الحالة
Offer[ur]: آپ کی علالت کے دوران میں آپ کی قسط کم کر سکتی ہوں
Offer[ur]: میں دیکھ سکتی ہوں کہ آپ کے اکاؤنٹ پر کوئی ایسا تحفظ ہے جو اس میں مدد دے
Where serious illness has reduced a customer's income or capacity to manage a
credit agreement, offer a reduced instalment while their situation settles,
without requiring medical evidence beyond a single confirmation. Check whether a
credit life or critical illness benefit attaches to the facility. Do not require
the customer to repeat the diagnosis to another team.

## Cross-cutting: collections conduct

### VP-J14 — Collections conduct and contact hours
Journeys: arrears_collections, forbearance_request
Products: personal_loan, credit_card, mortgage, car_finance
Offer: I can stop the collections calls while we sort out a plan
Offer: I can make sure nobody contacts your employer, your sponsor or your family about this
Offer: I can note that you should only be called between nine in the morning and eight at night
Offer[ar]: يمكنني إيقاف مكالمات التحصيل إلى أن نتفق على خطة
Offer[ar]: يمكنني التأكد من عدم اتصال أي شخص بصاحب العمل أو الكفيل أو عائلتك بخصوص هذا الأمر
Offer[ar]: يمكنني تسجيل ملاحظة بأن يتم الاتصال بك فقط بين التاسعة صباحاً والثامنة مساءً
Offer[ur]: جب تک ہم کوئی منصوبہ طے کرتے ہیں، میں وصولی کی کالیں بند کر سکتی ہوں
Offer[ur]: میں یقینی بنا سکتی ہوں کہ اس بارے میں آپ کے آجر، کفیل یا خاندان سے کوئی رابطہ نہ کیا جائے
Offer[ur]: میں یہ نوٹ کر سکتی ہوں کہ آپ کو صرف صبح نو سے رات آٹھ بجے کے درمیان کال کی جائے
Under the CBUAE Consumer Protection Standards and this bank's approved treatment
strategy, collections contact is permitted only between 09:00 and 20:00 Gulf
Standard Time, never during Friday prayers, and must never involve intimidation,
humiliation or persistent calling. It must never be directed at the customer's
employer, sponsor, relatives or neighbours: in a market where residency follows
employment, a call to an employer is not an embarrassment, it is a threat to the
customer's right to remain in the country, and it will be treated as misconduct.
Do not reference a police case, a travel ban, or the presentation of a security
cheque as leverage while a relief review is open. Where a customer reports such
contact, log it as a complaint the same day, suspend agency activity on the
account, and confirm in writing that it has stopped. A customer who is being
harassed will not engage with a repayment plan, so stopping the contact comes
before discussing the money.

### VP-J15 — Salary transfer, end of employment and residency
Journeys: arrears_collections, affordability_shock, forbearance_request
Products: personal_loan, credit_card, car_finance
Offer: I can hold the account while we confirm what has happened to your salary
Offer: I can look at what your end-of-service payment covers before we agree anything
Offer[ar]: يمكنني تعليق الحساب إلى أن نتأكد مما حدث لراتبك
Offer[ar]: يمكنني النظر في ما تغطيه مكافأة نهاية الخدمة قبل أن نتفق على أي شيء
Offer[ur]: میں اکاؤنٹ روک سکتی ہوں جب تک ہم یہ معلوم کر لیں کہ آپ کی تنخواہ کے ساتھ کیا ہوا
Offer[ur]: کسی بھی بات پر اتفاق سے پہلے میں دیکھ سکتی ہوں کہ آپ کی اینڈ آف سروس رقم کیا پوری کرتی ہے
On a salary-transfer facility, a missed instalment usually means a missed salary,
not a refusal to pay. Before any collections step, check whether the salary
credit arrived; where it did not, treat the account as an income shock and not as
a delinquency, and note that the Wage Protection System may be the customer's
route to recover unpaid wages from an employer. Where employment has ended,
establish what the end-of-service gratuity and final settlement cover before
proposing any arrangement, and set the plan against the customer's real remaining
time in the country rather than against a standard term. **Never tell a customer
that their visa, their residency or their ability to remain is at risk because of
this debt.** It may be an anxiety they already hold, and it is not this bank's to
confirm, imply or use.

### VP-J16 — Credit reporting, told plainly and in advance
Journeys: arrears_collections, forbearance_request, general_servicing
Offer: I can tell you exactly what will be reported to the credit bureau, and when
Offer: I can confirm in writing what the arrangement will look like on your record
Offer[ar]: يمكنني أن أخبرك بالضبط بما سيتم إبلاغه لمكتب الاتحاد للمعلومات الائتمانية ومتى
Offer[ar]: يمكنني أن أؤكد لك كتابياً كيف سيظهر هذا الاتفاق في سجلك الائتماني
Offer[ur]: میں آپ کو بالکل بتا سکتی ہوں کہ کریڈٹ بیورو کو کیا اور کب رپورٹ کیا جائے گا
Offer[ur]: میں تحریری طور پر تصدیق کر سکتی ہوں کہ یہ انتظام آپ کے ریکارڈ میں کیسے نظر آئے گا
A customer is entitled to know, before agreeing to anything, what will be
reported to Al Etihad Credit Bureau and when. Explain the reporting position for
the arrangement being offered in plain language, and confirm it in writing.
Never use a credit-bureau entry as a threat, and never imply that agreeing to a
plan on this call will remove a record that has already been made. A customer who
agrees to a plan without understanding its effect on their record has not given
informed agreement, whatever the recording shows they said.

## Cross-cutting: sales, suitability and support

### VP-J11 — Do not sell into vulnerability
Journeys: product_sale, affordability_shock, gambling_harm
Offer: I won't try to sell you anything today
Offer: I can look properly at whether that's right for you before anything happens
Offer[ar]: لن أحاول بيع أي شيء لك اليوم
Offer[ar]: يمكنني دراسة ما إذا كان ذلك مناسباً لك قبل اتخاذ أي خطوة
Offer[ur]: میں آج آپ کو کچھ بیچنے کی کوشش نہیں کروں گی
Offer[ur]: کوئی بھی قدم اٹھانے سے پہلے میں اچھی طرح دیکھ سکتی ہوں کہ آیا یہ آپ کے لیے مناسب ہے
Where a vulnerability signal is present, stop any sales or upgrade process for
this contact. Do not present further borrowing as a solution to unaffordability.
If the customer initiates a product request, complete an affordability and
suitability check that accounts for their circumstances and respects the debt
burden ratio, and record the reason for proceeding or declining.

### VP-J12 — Complaints and vulnerability
Journeys: complaint
Offer: I can look after this myself so you're not passed around
Offer: I can explain how to take this further if you're not happy with the outcome
Offer[ar]: يمكنني متابعة هذا الأمر بنفسي حتى لا يتم تحويلك من شخص إلى آخر
Offer[ar]: يمكنني أن أشرح لك كيفية تصعيد الأمر إذا لم تكن راضياً عن النتيجة
Offer[ur]: میں خود اس معاملے کو دیکھ سکتی ہوں تاکہ آپ کو ادھر ادھر نہ بھیجا جائے
Offer[ur]: اگر آپ نتیجے سے مطمئن نہ ہوں تو میں بتا سکتی ہوں کہ اسے آگے کیسے لے جانا ہے
Where a customer complains and a vulnerability signal is present, prioritise the

customer's needs. Explain the internal complaint route and then Sanadak, the
independent Ombudsman Unit, in plain language and without discouragement.
Consider whether distress and inconvenience should be recognised in the outcome.

### VP-J13 — Recording, consent and re-contact
Journeys: general_servicing, forbearance_request, bereavement_estate, fraud_scam
Offer: I can note this so the next person you speak to already knows
Offer: I'll only share this with the team who need it — is that alright?
Offer[ar]: يمكنني تسجيل ذلك حتى يعرفه الشخص التالي الذي تتحدث إليه
Offer[ar]: لن أشارك هذا إلا مع الفريق الذي يحتاج إليه — هل هذا مقبول لديك؟
Offer[ur]: میں یہ نوٹ کر سکتی ہوں تاکہ اگلے شخص کو پہلے سے معلوم ہو
Offer[ur]: میں یہ صرف اُس ٹیم کو بتاؤں گی جسے ضرورت ہے — کیا یہ ٹھیک ہے؟
Record the vulnerability signal, the adaptation offered, and the customer's
response, using the customer's own words where possible. Where the call was held
in a language other than English, record the language it was held in and the
approved wording used, so a reviewer can read what the customer actually heard.
Explain what will be recorded and why, and ask before sharing it beyond the team
that needs it. Set a review date so a temporary adaptation is revisited rather
than left to lapse, and make sure the next handler sees it so the customer never
has to explain twice.
